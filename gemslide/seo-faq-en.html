💎 Gem Slide

A spatial planning game — slide gems into target spots with the shortest route.

Is Gem Slide completely free?

Yes. Gem Slide is completely free, with no in-app purchases, no forced ads, and no account required. You can also upload an avatar, set a name, and write a catchphrase to make your own companion.

Do I need to download or install it?

No. Open it in a browser to play. It also supports PWA install — add it to your home screen on mobile or desktop like a native app and play offline after installation.

What languages are supported?

Supports three languages:

  • Simplified Chinese (default)
  • Traditional Chinese（zh-TW）
  • English

You can switch languages anytime in Settings on the home screen; all UI, tutorials, and coach dialogues follow the selection.

How do I play Gem Slide?

Gem Slide is A spatial planning game — slide gems into target spots with the shortest route. The in-game illustrated tutorial guides you from level 1 through the core techniques.

Mainly trains：spatial planning, forward thinking, and focus。

Who is it for? Can kids and seniors play?

Suitable for all ages.Gem Slide uses a 10-level difficulty loop (easy → normal → hard/Boss), so beginners start simple and progress gradually:

  • Kids → build foundational logic and rule understanding
  • Adults → activate the brain during a 5-minute break
  • Seniors → daily cognitive maintenance to keep the mind active

The in-game illustrated tutorial makes it easy to pick up.

How long should I play each day?

We recommend 5–10 minutes a day.The app tracks your daily clears and streak days, so moderate training pays off. The design is short-and-frequent, not binge-play — no infinite scroll, no forced notifications, just a healthy rhythm.

Will my data sync across devices?

Current progress is stored in browser LocalStorage (it will not be lost on the same device); cross-device cloud sync is not yet supported. Installing as a PWA means clearing browser data will not affect saved progress. Cloud save is planned for a future version.

Do brain-training games really work?

There is scientific evidence, but expectations should be realistic.

A large body of cognitive science research suggests that regular, varied cognitive training can help maintain and improve the brain's cognitive reserve. Two keys matter:

  • Consistency：a few minutes daily beats two hours once a week
  • Variety：different challenges stimulate different brain areas

Gem Slide trains your brain through spatial planning, forward thinking, and focus, The 10-level loop ensures progressive challenge. Effects vary by individual; treat it as part of a healthy brain routine, not a medical substitute.
