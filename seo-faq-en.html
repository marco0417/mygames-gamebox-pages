Is it free?

Yes. All 10 games are completely free. No in-app purchases, no forced ads, and no account required.

Do I need to download or install it?

No download needed. Open your browser and play instantly. You can also install it as a PWA on mobile or desktop for an app-like icon and offline play.

Which languages are supported?

Three languages:

  • Simplified Chinese
  • Traditional Chinese (zh-TW)
  • English

Switch anytime in Settings. The interface, tutorials, and companion dialog all follow your choice.

What does “Create Your Buddy” mean?

MindMate lets you create your own companion: upload avatars for each emotion, give them a name, write a catchphrase, and pick a home background. Your buddy reacts and cheers you on throughout the app.

Which games are included and what do they train?

Ten games across six cognitive dimensions:

  • FindACow → Logic & observation
  • Nonogram → Logic & spatial thinking
  • Gem Slide → Strategy & observation
  • Lights Out → Strategy & logic
  • Pipe Flow → Strategy & spatial thinking
  • Memory → Working memory & spatial thinking
  • Mambo Logic → Logic & working memory
  • Spot It → Observation & spatial thinking
  • Number Sense → Number sense & working memory
  • Sumplete → Number sense & logic

Who is it for?

All ages. A 10-level difficulty cycle (easy → normal → hard/Boss) adapts to beginners and experienced players. Each game has its own tutorial.

How long should I play each day?

5–10 minutes is enough. The daily radar tracks six dimensions (logic, spatial, strategy, memory, observation, number sense). The app is designed for short, healthy sessions—no infinite scrolling and no push notifications.

Does my progress sync across devices?

Progress is currently stored in your browser’s LocalStorage, so it stays on the same device. Cloud save may be added in a future update.
